# OSINT Evidence Collection
## Purpose
Collect authorized public-source evidence with citations, timestamps, and confidence.
## When to use
Use for scoped domains, organizations, public infrastructure, and source comparison.
## Required inputs
Authorization, research questions, targets, and exclusions.
## Permitted tools
Public web reads and scoped case-evidence writes.
## Prohibited actions
No intrusion, impersonation, credential access, or private-source collection.
## Execution steps
Define scope, collect primary sources, preserve provenance, compare, and validate.
## Evidence requirements
Capture source URL, access time, excerpt summary, and confidence.
## Output format
Cited report, evidence index, contradictions, and limitations.
## Validation and rollback
Revalidate volatile sources; evidence is never silently altered.
